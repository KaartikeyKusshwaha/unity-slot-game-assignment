## Applied direction
Immediate click feedback; a mechanical pull and separate bottom latch; reel ticks tied to real symbol travel; a motor that changes level as reels stop; three rising reveal accents; brief cabinet/reel compression and settling; count-up ticks and a distinct coin-collection finish. No spoken voice and no reward cues for a loss. Fair outcomes and exact accounting are independent of this presentation.

The original simple sine-tone effects were replaced with processed recordings and musical stingers. `Tools/build_audio.py` reproduces the mixed WAVs from the included source OGGs. All third-party source licenses are retained under Assets/Sounds/Kenney. New cues peak below full scale; final listening and mix approval remains a user review item.
